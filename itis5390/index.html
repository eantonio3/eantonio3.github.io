Project Overview

Hello, my name is Efren Antonio, and this site showcases my work in secure programming and penetration testing through a series of project reports completed as part of my coursework in ITIS 5390 - Capstone Interaction Design Project at UNC Charlotte.

My Contributions

In this capstone project, I played a key role in designing and developing a user-centric application focused on enhancing cybersecurity awareness. My contributions included conducting user research to identify pain points, creating wireframes and prototypes, and implementing secure coding practices to ensure the application's resilience against common vulnerabilities. Additionally, I collaborated closely with team members to integrate feedback and iteratively improve the user experience throughout the development process.

My Journey

This capstone project has been a transformative experience in my academic journey. It allowed me to apply theoretical knowledge from my coursework in a practical setting, honing my skills in user-centered design and secure software development. Throughout the project, I faced challenges that required creative problem-solving and adaptability, ultimately leading to personal and professional growth. This experience has solidified my passion for cybersecurity and user experience design, motivating me to pursue further opportunities in these fields.
