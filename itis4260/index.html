Home

Hello, my name is Efren Antonio, and this site showcases my work in cybersecurity analytics through a series of project reports completed as part of my coursework in ITIS 4260 - Introduction to Security Analytics at UNC Charlotte.

Each lab focuses on security related sense-making and decision-making based on data analytics techniques. Topics include: data cleaning and storage techniques, introduction to R, clustering analysis and statistical inference for security; log analysis; event correlation, anomaly detection, cyber threat intelligence, and use of public cybersecurity information resources (OSINT).

My goal with this portfolio is to provide a clear, professional record of my security analysis skills and methodologies, while also making the material accessible for others who are interested in this field of security.
